### Added
- **`tools/bench_reasoning_batch ... pad` and `eval_decision_batched_reasoning.py --pad`
  keep every batched step at exactly the batch size (#236).**
  - A finished question's slot keeps decoding, with its tokens discarded,
    until the next question takes it.
  - Spare slots decode padding.
  - Each question's tokens therefore come from same-size
    `geist_session_decode_batch` steps, which makes them independent of the
    other members bit for bit.
  - The #236 amendment in `docs/DECISION_CAMPAIGN_PLAN.json` uses it to
    re-run, at a cap of 8192 tokens, only the questions that cap 2048 cut
    short (and those decoded in smaller tail batches).
