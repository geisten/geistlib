### Fixed

- Metal: the per-key split-KV decode kernel (`attention_dec_f16` / `_f32kv`, taken for one query row when head_dim is not a multiple of 32) is correct below head_dim 32 again. Its first 32-lane chunk was unguarded: the lanes past head_dim read Q from unwritten threadgroup memory and K/V of the next head (past the cache at its last row) and wrote their partial into the next simdgroup's slots, so decode returned wrong and nondeterministic values, at times NaN. Real models (head_dim >= 64) were not affected; the Qwen3.5 test fixture (head_dim 16) made `test_decision_prefix_unit` flake on "prefix + one suffix token at exact capacity" with "non-finite selected logit". The Metal parity test now covers decode at head_dim 16 and 8, and the decision check compares the exact-capacity answer with a fresh prefill bit for bit (#271).
